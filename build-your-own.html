Organization and Indexing

John Locke's Index Method

John Locke's index method was designed with the purpose of accesibility and making it easier for readers to find/reference their notes at later dates. His method included different elements such as...

  • 2-page index grid, where the first two pages were left for organizing a grid consisting of each letter of the alphabet, divided into the 5 vowels.
  • Each page is categorized by date on the left margin. These entries are logged into the index grid.
