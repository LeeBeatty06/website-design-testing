What is a Commonplace Book?

A commonplace book is a personal notebook used to compile information, quotations, ideas, observations, and drawings gathered from various sources and compiled into a single volume. This practice has been used for centuries as a way to preserve and organize thoughts, inspirations, and knowledge.

Why Create a Commonplace Book?

Filtering Information

Keeping a commonplace book encourages you to collect and filter information that resonates with you. Rather than simply hoarding information, it gives you a place to limit, organize, and presere the ideas you find most meaningful.
